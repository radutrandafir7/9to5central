The Daily Dose of Happiness

Guided mindfulness, one card a day. For iPhone.

One small card a day for a calmer you: breathe slowly, stretch, take a short walk, write three good things. A calm voice guides every step, and every card shows the published research behind it. Made from our printed 48-card deck.

  • Twelve cards are free, two in every theme. All 48 cards and the Home Screen widgets are one purchase. No subscription.
  • No account, no streaks, no AI inside. Your journal stays on your iPhone.

Help

I bought the deck on another iPhone. Open Settings (the gear on Today) and tap Restore purchase.

The guidance stops when I take my headphones out. That’s on purpose: the practice pauses, so nothing plays out loud by surprise. Tap Resume to go on.

I don’t hear the end chime on a walk. Walks and other phone-away cards ring their end with a notification. Allow notifications for Daily Dose in your iPhone’s Settings. The power nap uses an alarm that rings even in silent mode.

Apple Health. Finished cards can add Mindful Minutes. Turn it on in the app’s Settings; turn it off in the Health app.

Moving to a new iPhone. Your journal moves with your iPhone backup. You can also export it as text from the Journal tab.
