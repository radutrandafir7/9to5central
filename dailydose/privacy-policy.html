Privacy Policy

The Daily Dose of Happiness for iPhone — Last updated: November 2026

1. The Short Version

The Daily Dose of Happiness does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

2. What Stays on Your iPhone

Your journal, how you felt before and after each card, your lists, goals, notes, well-being check answers and settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. You can lock them with Face ID. They are removed if you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone, for example with iCloud Backup, that backup is handled by Apple and may include the app’s data. You can export your journal as text yourself from the Journal tab.

3. Apple Health

If you turn it on, the app writes Mindful Minutes to Apple Health for the cards you finish. It never reads anything from Apple Health.

4. Camera, Motion and Alarms

  • The camera is used only for one card’s live mirror. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Motion data is used only to count your steps during a walk, on your iPhone.
  • The power nap sets an alarm on your iPhone.

Each permission is asked only when you first use the feature, and you can say no.

5. Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details.

6. Internet Use

The app connects only to the App Store, for purchases. It has no servers of its own.

7. Notifications

Reminders and end chimes are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

8. Children’s Privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

If anything here changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

10. Contact

Reach out anytime. We reply. — 9to5central@gmail.com (we use your email address only to answer you).
